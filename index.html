How I work

Three calls I make on every project

Put AI where it pays

AI takes the per-customer work nobody can afford to do by hand. Fixed rules set every price and payout, and a person makes the calls that need one.

Fix the ground truth before blaming the model

In my brand benchmark, most failures came from incomplete reference data, not AI error. Fixing the data turned 13 of 14 failures into passes.

Let the data decide what continues

I retired a forecasting signal once 1,444 simulated decisions showed no edge, before any money was at risk.
